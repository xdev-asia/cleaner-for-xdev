Privacy Policy

Cleaner for xDev · Effective 9 November 2026

Cleaner for xDev does not collect, store or share any personal data. This policy covers the Mac App Store edition and the edition downloaded from GitHub.

What the app reads

The app reads the names, sizes and dates of files in the folders you scan, to show where your disk space goes. To find duplicate files it reads file contents and compares their checksums on your Mac. None of this leaves your Mac, and the app keeps no record of it after you quit.

Network

The app makes no network connections. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting of its own.

Apple Intelligence

When you ask the app to explain an item or summarise a scan, it sends names, paths and sizes, never file contents, to Apple's on-device language model. The model runs on your Mac and the app sends nothing to Apple or to us.

Crash reports from Apple

If you share analytics with app developers in System Settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. Apple's privacy policy covers that data.

Changes and contact

We will post any change to this policy on this page. Questions: open an issue at github.com/xdev-asia/cleaner-for-xdev.
